# Sepal before and now, gpuix-svelte and Tauri 2 (1,000 issues)

Apple M4 Pro, macOS 27.0.1, the Tracker bench build, window 1200x800 at 2x. Cells: median (min–max). Sepal now and Tauri were measured in one session, interleaved run by run (`final`, 1 warm-up + 3 runs each); Sepal old and gpuix come from `mem-1000` (23 Nov, 1 warm-up + 3 runs). Sessions on different days drift by up to ~15%. Generated by `make_compare_report.py` from the raw data in `compare/results/raw/`, which is on the `tracker-compare` branch only (main does not have it).

‡ **WindowServer GPU time cannot separate the apps.** It is macOS's WindowServer, which composites every window on screen, read from the same counter (`accumulatedGPUTime`). In the idle runs, where neither app draws a frame, Sepal and Tauri differ by 590 ms: more than either app's own GPU time in any scenario (at most 566 ms). The harness is not what adds it: these runs did not record the screen (`--no-record`), and with an idle Sepal Tracker window open and no harness running, WindowServer spent 382 ms per second, as in the runs (~400), against 76 ms per second with no Tracker window and the display kept awake. It is what such a window on screen costs WindowServer on this Mac (5K display, Stage Manager on); whether any window costs that was not measured. Recorded from 24 Nov on: — for the 23 Nov columns.

**Sepal's memory after it goes idle comes in two sizes.** It lets go of its kept frame and depth buffer 2 s after its last frame (2.01-2.02 s after load in 12 of 12 runs of idle, dialog, scroll and views; the clock never goes 2 s without a frame). What remains 5 s after load is either 28-30 MB of GPU memory and 94-98 MB of RAM (6 runs) or 43 and 112-113 MB (6 runs), from launch to launch: the window's surface keeps one or two 2400x1600 drawables (15 MB each) resident (footprint's IOSurface category). At the end of the idle runs: 88 MB of RAM with one, 107 MB with two.

**GPU memory while active is higher than on 23 Nov** (clock +7 MB, dialog +15 MB, views +16 MB, now against old, at the end of the scenario). About 15 MB of that is the kept frame, the 2400x1600 BGRA8 texture damage tracking draws each frame over (the old build drew straight into the window's surface); it is let go 2 s after the last frame. The rest of GPU memory is mostly the driver's, which varies between sessions.

**gpuix-svelte is a port** (its own widgets, a CSS subset); Tauri runs the same components and CSS as Sepal. Tauri's numbers include its WebKit WebContent, GPU and Networking processes.

**Why Tauri was missing before:** the `mem-1000` session was stopped on request on 23 Nov at 21:08 IST, right after Tauri's three idle runs; its other scenarios had not started (no log or record exists for them). It was not a failure of Tauri's runs. Every scenario ran here.
