# Logo options (second round)

Three candidates, left to right in `options.png`. Each row of that sheet
shows the mark, the mark as an app icon, and the mark at 64 and 32 pixels.

1. **Lobes and bolt** (`option-1.svg`): a two-lobed S in Svelte orange with a
   white lightning bolt through it.
2. **Split** (`option-2.svg`): a rounded tile struck in two by a lightning
   bolt; the halves read as the lobes of an S. It is its own app icon.
3. **Two charges** (`option-3.svg`): the upper lobe in Svelte orange, the lower
   in electric yellow, and a lightning-shaped gap where they meet.

`option-N-icon.svg` and `.png` are the app-icon versions: 1024px, with the
margin macOS expects, ready to be used as `static/icon.png`.

The first round (three plain S shapes) was rejected and is not kept.

## About the Svelte logo

These are original drawings; none contains or modifies the Svelte logo file.
Options 1 and 3 are deliberately shaped like it, though: a tilted S made of
two rounded lobes, in Svelte's orange. Option 2 is the one that stands on its
own.

That matters because the Svelte branding repository
(github.com/sveltejs/branding) has no licence file, only conditions of usage.
They cover the logo and its colour scheme, and ask that use does not suggest
the Svelte project sponsors or endorses another project, and that use to
indicate compatibility is accurate and in good faith. A logo that looks like a
relative of Svelte's is closer to that line than one that does not. If option
1 or 3 is chosen, it is worth asking the Svelte maintainers.

`previous-icon.svg` is the placeholder icon the examples have used so far.
